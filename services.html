SERVICES

Community Development Strategy

Community Asset Brokers helps businesses and developers understand and navigate the community and governmental processes that can influence whether a project advances smoothly, encounters delays, or becomes unnecessarily adversarial.

Our work is tailored to the needs of each project. We help clients identify potential issues early, understand the people and institutions involved, and develop a practical strategy for moving forward.

Community Engagement

Identify neighborhood stakeholders, anticipate community concerns, organize outreach, and develop strategies for productive engagement.

Development Process Navigation

Help clients understand the local processes surrounding zoning, permitting, licensing, public notification, hearings, and governmental approvals.

Project Strategy

Evaluate a project's community and governmental environment and develop a practical strategy for timing, outreach, approvals, and risk.

Public & Stakeholder Communication

Assist with clear communication to residents, neighborhood organizations, public officials, and other stakeholders affected by a proposed project.

Every project is different.
