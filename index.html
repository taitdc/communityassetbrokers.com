About Us

The Twin Cities Metro is a labyrinth of rules and regulations intended to balance the economic motivations of developers with the comprehensive plans of the neighborhoods in which they invest.

The system is never as clear and inviting as advertised. Community Asset Brokers was created out of a need for both for-profit and nonprofit commercial and residential developers to efficiently navigate community, city, and county procedures.

What We Do

The procedures of local government and their departments can be confusing and burdensome. Adding the layer of community notification can send businesses into unfamiliar territory without direct assistance.

We bridge the gap between community planning expectations and business realities.

We assist clients with zoning changes, major and minor variances, conditional use permits, historic preservation, site plan review, licenses, and other projects requiring community notification and input.

Services

We engage early in the development process so community input can be integrated without compromising project goals, timelines, and viability.

Ideas & Financing

We troubleshoot potential conflicts with neighborhood plans and politics during the early stages of a project, assist with community engagement, and research financing opportunities that may intersect with the project location.

City & County Applications

We help clients navigate community-notification requirements and engage residents through outreach, petitions, organizational committees, and community meetings.

Approval

We organize community supporters and help translate community enthusiasm into effective participation during public hearings and governmental approval processes.

Appeal

When an application is denied, we help broaden community support, develop public-relations strategies, and prepare for appeals to higher authorities.
