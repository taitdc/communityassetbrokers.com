Navigating Development Starts With Understanding the Community

The Twin Cities Metro is a labyrinth of rules and regulations that are meant to synergize the economic motivations of developers with the comprehensive plans of the neighborhoods in which they invest. The "system" is never as clear and inviting as advertised. Community Asset Brokers was created out of a need for both for-profit and nonprofit commercial and residential developers to successfully navigate community, city, and county procedures.
