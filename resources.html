RESOURCES

Understanding the Development Process

Successful development requires more than understanding a site or a business plan. Projects operate within a network of community expectations, governmental processes, public notice requirements, and local decision-making.

These resources are intended to help developers and businesses better understand the environment in which projects move forward.

Community Engagement

Understanding who is affected by a project, how neighborhood organizations operate, and when community engagement should begin.

Public Process

Understanding public notification, hearings, governmental review, and the points where community concerns can influence a project's progress.

Project Preparation

Identifying potential concerns early and preparing a project for conversations with residents, public officials, neighborhood organizations, and other stakeholders.

Local Government

Understanding the roles of city and county departments, elected officials, planning bodies, and other institutions involved in development decisions.

Resources will continue to be added.

Community Asset Brokers will use this section to provide practical information about community engagement, local government processes, and development strategy in the Twin Cities Metro.
